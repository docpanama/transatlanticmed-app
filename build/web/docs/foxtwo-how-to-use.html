How to Use FoxTwo — CCT_EDGE Transport Simulator

Please read this entire guide first, then read the student instructions.

Purpose

This simulation evaluates your ability to manage a critically ill or injured patient during pre-transport stabilization and rotary-wing transport in a resource-constrained environment.

It evaluates:

  • Preflight stabilization discipline
  • Transport readiness assessment
  • Recognition of time-sensitive pathology
  • Decision-making under time pressure
  • Management during patient movement and flight
  • Ventilation, oxygenation, and hemodynamics in transport
  • Packaging and monitoring strategy
  • Responsible integration of AI as a clinical decision-support tool

Overview

In CCT_EDGE, you will complete a dynamic transport simulation using FoxTwo as both:

  • Simulation engine
  • Clinical decision-support tool

You will manage a patient from initial bedside assessment through stabilization for transport, in-transit management, or clinical deterioration. The patient may improve, deteriorate, or die based on your decisions. There are no scripted time points.

Time Model

This is a time-compressed simulation of transport care. Each reassessment represents a clinically meaningful interval.

Time will NOT advance unless you explicitly request it. You must clearly signal transitions such as:

  • “I reassess the patient”
  • “Advance time”
  • “We load the patient”
  • “We take off”

The simulation responds only to your actions.

Instructions

Step 1 — Select a Scenario

Choose one scenario. The vignette for your scenario will be shown to you in the app:

  • Easy
  • Hard
  • Peds

Step 2 — Open the App and Start a New Session

Open the FoxTwo link and start a new session. Do not reuse an existing session.

Step 3 — Enter Your Certification and Experience Level

Before the scenario begins, the app will ask for:

  • Your certification level
  • Your critical care transport experience level

This step applies to scenarios. If you chose clinical decision support without a scenario, there is no Step 3 — the tool opens straight away and you ask your question.

Important: FoxTwo is running the CCT_EDGE simulation engine. FoxTwo is also functioning as your clinical decision-support tool. You may ask clinical questions, request guideline-based reasoning, and use it to structure your thinking. This simulation evaluates AI-assisted critical care transport decision-making.

Step 4 — Begin Management

Provide an initial structured assessment including:

  • Problem list or working diagnoses
  • Immediate priorities
  • Transport readiness assessment
  • Initial intervention plan
  • Monitoring strategy
  • Contingency planning

Then:

  • Reassess explicitly
  • Advance time deliberately
  • Track trends
  • Document decisions clearly

You retain full clinical authority.

AI Use Policy

You may:

  • Ask clinical knowledge questions
  • Request guideline-based reasoning
  • Use the model to structure documentation

You must NOT:

  • Delegate decision authority
  • Assume interventions occurred without stating them
  • Allow AI to replace your structured reasoning

This simulation evaluates how you integrate AI responsibly in transport care.

Scenario Termination

The scenario ends when:

  • The patient is stabilized for transport
  • The patient deteriorates or dies
  • You declare the exercise complete

After Action Report (AAR) Generation

At the end of the simulation, FoxTwo will present your full written After Action Report (AAR) in chat. When it says your AAR is ready, approve the next prompt and your downloadable .docx file will be generated. Your report will be available immediately.

The report contains no personally identifying information (PII). If you are willing, we ask that you send a copy of your AAR to prolonged.sim@gmail.com or delaneym@transatlanticmed.com. We want to see how you did! We also welcome any feedback; your comments help us make CCT_EDGE the best AI scenario simulator we can so you can save lives.

Your report is deleted along with your scenario when you close your session.
