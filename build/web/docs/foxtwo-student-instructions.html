FoxTwo / CCT_EDGE — Student Instructions

What Is This?

FoxTwo is a guideline-informed educational and clinical reasoning support tool for critical care transport (CCT) professionals working in dynamic, resource-constrained, high-risk environments — where resuscitation and movement happen at the same time.

CCT_EDGE is the simulation engine built into FoxTwo. When you start a scenario, FoxTwo becomes the simulator: it presents the patient, responds to your actions, and tracks how the patient changes through packaging, loading, and flight.

FoxTwo runs as a web app. Open the link — no account or sign-in is needed. For background, read What is FoxTwo? For the full guide, read How to Use FoxTwo.

How a Session Works

  1. Open the link to the FoxTwo app and start a new session.
  2. Pick a scenario:
    • Easy
    • Hard
    • Peds
  3. Enter your certification level and your critical care transport experience level. This step applies to scenarios — clinical decision support without a scenario asks nothing and opens straight away.
  4. The vignette for your scenario appears. Read it — it is all you know about the patient to start with.
  5. Manage the patient by typing your actions and decisions. Give a structured assessment first — problem list, priorities, transport readiness, intervention plan, monitoring strategy, and contingency planning — then keep managing as the situation changes. If you want FoxTwo’s guidance, ask for it directly — it is your clinical decision-support tool inside the simulation as well as the simulator itself.
  6. Time advances only when you ask it to. Say “I reassess the patient”, “advance time”, “we load the patient”, or “we take off”. Each reassessment represents a clinically meaningful interval.
  7. When the scenario ends — the patient is stabilized for transport, the patient deteriorates or dies, or you declare the exercise complete — FoxTwo generates your After Action Report as a downloadable .docx file.

Your Report

Your After Action Report contains no identifying information about you. We would really appreciate it if you emailed a copy to prolonged.sim@gmail.com or delaneym@transatlanticmed.com — we want to see how you did, how CCT_EDGE did, and how we can improve critical care transport training and AI clinical decision tools. You can ignore the report; it is deleted along with your scenario when you close your session. It is simply a description of the care you rendered.

Send us any feedback you want about your experience!

FoxTwo is a tool intended to support clinical decision-making in critical care transport. CCT_EDGE was conceived and designed by the CCT_EDGE Team, a group of military aeromedical clinicians with experience and training in critical care transport.
